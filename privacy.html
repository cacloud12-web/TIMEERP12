Introduction

Last updated: July 2026. This policy explains how TimeERP (Law Seva Management Private Limited) collects and uses information when you visit our website, request a demo, contact sales, or use our cloud ERP services.

We are committed to handling personal and business information responsibly and in line with applicable privacy laws. By using TimeERP, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

database

Information We Collect

We may collect business contact details (name, email, phone, company), product usage data, and information you submit via demo or contact forms.

Depending on how you interact with TimeERP, this may also include:

  • Account and profile information for authorized users
  • Communications you send to our sales or support teams
  • Technical logs such as IP address, browser type, and device information
settings

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate, secure, and improve TimeERP. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Provide and improve TimeERP services
  • Respond to demo requests and support inquiries
  • Send product and service communications you request
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
encrypted

Data Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls appropriate to enterprise software.

Access to personal information is limited to personnel and partners who need it to operate, support, or secure our services, and who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

hub

Third-Party Services

TimeERP may rely on trusted infrastructure and service providers for hosting, analytics, communications, and customer support. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their services to us.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with third parties only when necessary to provide TimeERP, comply with law, or with your consent.

gavel

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

schedule

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide TimeERP services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, and meet legal or regulatory requirements.

When information is no longer required, we take steps to delete or anonymize it in accordance with our retention practices.

child_care

Children's Privacy

TimeERP is a business platform and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
